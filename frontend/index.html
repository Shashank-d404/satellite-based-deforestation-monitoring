How the Detection Works

Sentinel-2 Level-2A imagery is used to calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for 2025 and 2026.

Scene Classification Layer (SCL) filtering removes unsuitable cloud and shadow classes before comparing the two years.

Pixels with an NDVI decrease below -0.20 are flagged as potential vegetation loss.
