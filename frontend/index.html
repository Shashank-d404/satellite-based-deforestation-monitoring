ABOUT FOREST MONITOR

A hackathon prototype designed to connect observation with action.

Forest Monitor combines satellite imagery, cloud-aware preprocessing, NDVI change analysis and interactive geospatial visualization in one workflow. The current prototype focuses on transparent, reproducible screening rather than claiming confirmed deforestation from a single signal.

Open technology stack Public satellite data Explainable workflow
AI roadmap

The current MVP is satellite processing + NDVI change screening. A future AI/ML layer can classify change patterns and prioritize reports once suitable labeled training data is available.
